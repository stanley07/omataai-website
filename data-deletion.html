Data Deletion Instructions

Effective: 16 October 2026

Individuals and authorized business representatives can request deletion of personal information associated with Omata AI or the Voice Ledger pilot.

Step 1 — Submit a request

Email info@omataai.com.ng using the subject Omata AI Data Deletion Request. Describe the records involved, such as a website enquiry, WhatsApp conversation or merchant account, and give enough context to locate them. Do not include passwords, PINs or API tokens.

Step 2 — Verify authorization

To prevent unauthorized deletion, we may ask you to demonstrate control of the contact number or authorization to act for the affected business. Do not send identity documents unless we explain why they are necessary and provide an appropriate channel.

Step 3 — Review and response

We will review the request and advise what eligible information can be removed or anonymized. Some accounting, security, transaction or legal records may need to be retained where lawful or required. We will communicate the outcome or any further information needed.

Third-party copies

Deleting Omata AI records does not automatically remove information held independently by WhatsApp/Meta, message recipients, device backups or other providers. Their own privacy controls may apply.
