Privacy Policy

Effective: 16 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Autonomous Data Enterprise, operator of Omata AI and its Voice Ledger pilot, handles information provided through our website, business enquiries, and pilot service. Some features described below are in development and may not be available to all users.

Information we may collect

Depending on the service enabled and how you use it, we may handle names, business details, contact details, WhatsApp numbers, text and voice messages, transcriptions, proposed and confirmed transactions, stock or customer-balance data, and technical or security logs.

How information is used

We use relevant information to respond to enquiries, support pilot services, route communications to the correct merchant, interpret business instructions, prepare proposed entries for human review, maintain authorized records, troubleshoot delivery, and protect against abuse.

WhatsApp, voice and AI services

When relevant features are enabled, WhatsApp messages or voice recordings may be processed for transcription and AI-generated suggestions. External service providers used for messaging, hosting, transcription and AI processing may handle data needed to provide those functions. WhatsApp/Meta also has its own privacy practices. AI outputs can be inaccurate; proposed financial changes are intended to require human confirmation before posting.

Sharing

We may disclose information to service providers to operate the service, where required by law, or when needed to protect legal rights. Merchant business records are not intended to be publicly displayed.

Security and retention

We use appropriate administrative and technical controls for the pilot, including verification of incoming webhook signatures where configured. We keep data as needed for service delivery, operational security, applicable legal obligations or disputes. Retention duration may differ by record type and environment; this policy does not promise a fixed retention period.

Your choices

You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal data, subject to applicable laws and lawful record-retention requirements. See Data Deletion Instructions.

Children

Omata AI is designed for business use and is not directed to children.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the pilot evolves. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published. For privacy enquiries email info@omataai.com.ng.
