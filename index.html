How It Works

From conversation to business record.

Omata AI's Voice Ledger workflow is designed to make business recordkeeping easier while keeping people in control.

01Send a text or voice note
02AI prepares a structured suggestion
03A person reviews and confirms
04The system updates business records

WhatsApp integration and voice capabilities are under active pilot development. Financial transactions require human review before posting.
